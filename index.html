Our approach

Curiosity Without Easy Answers

Veil of the Unknown explores paranormal reports, folklore, unusual experiences, and mysteries with an open mind. We distinguish personal accounts and legends from verified facts, consider ordinary explanations, and never present unverified claims as proven evidence.
